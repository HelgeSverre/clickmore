ClickMore

One click.
Every button.

Place a floating trigger. Connect your click spots. One press clicks them all, wherever they are on your screen.

Download for macOS↓

macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon and Intel
Free · Signed & Apple-notarized

brew install --cask helgesverre/tap/clickmore

⌥ Drag to add

Pull a spot from the trigger and drop it on a button.

⌘ Move together

Move the trigger and every spot as one. Both shortcuts are rebindable.

Made for your Mac

A persistent overlay with VoiceOver support. Controls live in the menu bar.

Allow ClickMore in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility to click other apps.
